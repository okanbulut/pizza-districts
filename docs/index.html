The idea behind the game

Assignment ↔ Update

k-means repeats two steps: assign each house to its nearest centroid, then move each centroid to the mean of its cluster. Repeat until positions stop changing. You choose k; the algorithm only optimizes the locations.

🧠 The algorithm, in three moves

01 / ASSIGN
Find the nearest shop

Each house joins the cluster with the closest centroid.

02 / UPDATE
Move to the mean

Each shop moves to the average x and y of its assigned houses.

03 / REPEAT
Converge

Keep going until the shop locations stop changing.

📊 How you’re scored

Why not just minimize SSE? Adding stores never makes the best possible SSE worse, and with one store per house it reaches zero. So each city has a hidden intended number of stores that matches how its neighborhoods were generated. Model choice (50 pts): 50 for the intended k, 20 if you are off by one. Optimization (50 pts): full marks when your SSE matches the best reference SSE for the k you chose, falling to 0 at a 50% gap. Attempt 2 earns 85% and attempt 3 earns 70% of those points.
