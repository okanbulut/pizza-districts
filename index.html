The idea behind the game

Assignment ↔ Update

k-means repeats two steps: assign each house to its nearest centroid, then move each centroid to the mean of its cluster. Repeat until positions stop changing. This optimizes squared Euclidean distance—not necessarily the sum of raw distances.

🧠 The algorithm, in three moves

01 / ASSIGN
Find the nearest shop

Each house joins the cluster with the closest centroid.

02 / UPDATE
Move to the mean

Each shop moves to the average x and y of its assigned houses.

03 / REPEAT
Converge

Keep going until the shop locations stop changing.

📊 Researcher’s takeaway

Watch the objective after each iteration. K-means is sensitive to initialization: different starting positions can converge to different local minima. Compare your manual placement with auto-optimization.
